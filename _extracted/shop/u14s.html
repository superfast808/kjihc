AGE GROUPS

Players must have been born in the year 2011 at the earliest. U14 teams must have a minimum of 10 players and at least 1 netminder and a maximum overall of 17 players (Max 2 netminders). No guest players will be allowed unless agreed with the Tournament Committee in advance. NB: Playing up one age group will be allowed. Authorised playdowns will require the consent of the KJIHC Tournament Committee. Teams who arrive with less than the minimum bench size must notify the tournament committee ASAP.

TEAMS

Minimum bench size: 10 skaters and 1 netminder. Maximum bench size: 17 Players

Any team who arrive with less than 10 skaters or lose players due to illness/injury during the tournament will be allocated players. Every team should provide an official team list one week prior to the tournament and notify of any amendments to this upon arrival at the tournament.

FORMAT

Teams will play an initial set of games followed by a medal qualification stage. No puck warm-ups prior to the game and no huddle around the net. Please be ready to take the ice 5 minutes before your scheduled game time. The tournament may run ahead or behind schedule. We will make you aware if there is a substantial amendment to running times. Each game will be 25 minutes rolling clock. No timeouts allowed.

If teams are tied on equal points in the round robin competition, the qualifying placing will be decided upon:

  • Head-to-head records
  • Goal difference
  • Goals scored
  • Number of penalties received
  • If teams are still tied, the drawing of lots will determine placing.

GAME RULES

The semi-finals will be 25 minutes. There will be a running clock with the last minute of the game played on a stop clock. In the event of a draw, the taking of 3 penalty shots in accordance with IIHF rules will determine the winner. No timeouts allowed.

PRIZES

Trophies and player awards for Top 3 teams. All Star Team Awards (voted by KJIHC Tournament Committee).

BEHAVIOUR

It is the responsibility of each club to ensure that every member of their club is well-behaved (this includes Players, Coaches, Managers, Parents, and Supporters). The tournament organisers have the right to remove anyone from the tournament who is unruly or causes a disturbance. They also have the right to refuse entry to anyone whom they feel is undesirable and likely to cause problems.

IT IS PROHIBITED TO PLAY HOCKEY OFF THE ICE IN THE ENTIRE FACILITY. STICKS AND PUCKS MUST REMAIN IN DRESSING ROOMS. ALL DAMAGES MADE ON THE PREMISES OF THE GALLEON WILL BE THE RESPONSIBILITY OF OFFENDING INDIVIDUALS, AND TEAMS WILL BE OBLIGED TO PAY FOR THE DAMAGES WITHOUT DELAY.
